# Project Information

- **Project Title**: Olist Brazil Delivery Analytics & Intelligence Platform
- **Course**: AI For Business Analytics (5th Semester)
- **Institution**: Dayananda Sagar University (DSU)
- **Domain**: E-Commerce Logistics, Delivery Performance & Predictive Analytics
- **Dataset**: Brazilian E-Commerce Delivery Dataset by Olist (2017 - 2018)
- **Scope**: Production client-side intelligence platform featuring exploratory analytics, route optimization, automated anomaly detection, regression/classification ML models, geospatial routing, and interactive business simulation.
